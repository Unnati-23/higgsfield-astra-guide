Use Blender for all 3D objects and animation. Use Higgsfield for the final rendered video generation/compositing.

IMPORTANT:
This should NOT look like a cinematic factory documentary. It should look like a clean, minimal, high-end engineering visualization on a very light grey/white studio background.

Keep the camera mostly in an elevated 3/4 isometric perspective. Use soft shadows, physically plausible materials, extremely clean geometry, and smooth mechanical animation.

Keep this title permanently visible near the upper-left/top area:
"$400M Chipmaking Machine"
Use bold black sans-serif text.

Do not add narration, subtitles, people, decorative backgrounds or unnecessary graphics.

SHOT 6 - MULTI-LAYER OPTICAL TOWER
approximately 0:11.7-0:13.2

The wafer contains a very regular grid of hundreds of small pale-purple rectangular dies.
Camera slowly pushes closer.
The wafer should have a subtle reflective rim and realistic thickness.

SHOT 9 - EXTRACT ONE DIE
approximately 0:17.3-0:19.5
One small square die separates from the wafer.
The large wafer moves out of frame while the selected die remains floating in the center.
The die is a dark square containing a grid of smaller purple-grey cells.
Allow it to rotate slightly in 3D.

SHOT 10 - PLACE DIE ON GREEN PACKAGE
approximately 0:19.5-0:20.5
A green processor substrate/package appears beneath the die.
The die lowers smoothly onto the center of the green substrate.
Show tiny metallic contact details around the substrate edges.

SHOT 11 - PROCESSOR HEAT SPREADER
approximately 0:20.5-0:21.5

SHOT 12 - FINISHED PROCESSOR HERO SHOT
approximately 0:21.5-0:30
Show the completed desktop-style processor package.
Green PCB substrate.

Tiny gold/contact details.
Clean realistic product geometry.

"PROCESSOR"

Camera:

very slow subtle push-in
slight controlled rotation if needed
Hold this hero shot through the end.
